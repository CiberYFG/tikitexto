Bayern Munich facts and curiosities

Data as of 2 November 2026

Bayern Munich is Germany's most decorated club. Here are some of the most striking stories in its history, from the 1970s to today.

1

Founded in 1900

Bayern was founded on 27 February 1900 in Munich. For the first half of its history it was a regional club, and it did not win its first German championship until 1932.

2

Three European Cups in a row

With Beckenbauer, Gerd Müller and Sepp Maier, Bayern won the European Cup in 1974, 1975 and 1976, a continental hat-trick that only Real Madrid and Ajax had achieved before.

3

Gerd Müller, the «Bomber»

Gerd Müller scored 365 goals in 427 Bundesliga games, a competition record. In 1971–72 he scored 40 goals in a single league season, a mark that stood for almost fifty years.

4

Lewandowski breaks the record

In 2020–21, Robert Lewandowski scored 41 Bundesliga goals and broke Gerd Müller's record, which had stood since 1971–72.

5

The Kaiser, champion as player and coach

Franz Beckenbauer, nicknamed «the Kaiser», won the 1974 World Cup as West Germany's captain and the 1990 one as coach. He was a symbol of Bayern throughout his career.

6

The lost 1999 final

In the 1999 Champions League final in Barcelona, Bayern led Manchester United 1-0 until the 90th minute, but United scored twice in stoppage time and won 2-1.

Advertisement
7

The 2013 treble and the 2020 sextuple

In 2013 Bayern became the first German club to win the Bundesliga, the cup and the Champions League in one season. In 2020 they repeated the treble and, with the German Super Cup, the UEFA Super Cup and the Club World Cup (won in February 2021), completed a sextuple.

8

The 8-2 against Barcelona

On 14 August 2020 in Lisbon, Bayern beat Barcelona 8-2 in the Champions League quarter-finals, one of the heaviest scorelines in Champions League knockout football.

9

A stadium that changes colour

The Allianz Arena, opened in 2005, has a panelled façade that lights up red when Bayern play, white when the German national team plays and, until 2017, blue when the other Munich club, 1860, played there.

Bayern Munich in the European Cup, year by year

Bayern Munich has won the European Cup and Champions League 6 times: the first in 1974 and the latest, so far, in 2020. The longest gap between two titles was 25 years (1976–2001) and the shortest, 1 year (1974–1975). By decade: 1970s: 3, 2000s: 1, 2010s: 1, 2020s: 1. Frequently asked questions

How many European Cups did Bayern win in the 1970s?

Three in a row: 1974, 1975 and 1976.

What goals record does Gerd Müller hold?

365 goals in 427 Bundesliga games, a competition record.

What is Bayern's sextuple?

The six titles it won between 2020 and 2021: Bundesliga, cup, Champions League, German Super Cup, UEFA Super Cup and Club World Cup.
