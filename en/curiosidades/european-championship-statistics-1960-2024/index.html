European Championship statistics 1960–2024: champions, hosts, finals and growth of the tournament

Data as of 24 November 2026

TIKITEXTO's own study of the 17 European Championships: nations with most titles, hosts that won, finals decided in extra time or on penalties, goals per final and how it grew from 4 to 24 teams. With a filterable table.

The key findings in 6 numbers

  • Spain is the nation with most European Championships (4), followed by Germany (3) and, with 2 each, France and Italy (Germany includes West Germany).
  • Only 3 of 17 hosts lifted the trophy at home (1964, 1968, 1984), and 2 more lost a final they played as hosts (2004, 2016).
  • 4 finals needed extra time or a golden goal (1960, 1996, 2000, 2016) and 2 were decided on penalties (1976, 2020).
  • The average is 2.47 goals per final (42 in 17, counting the 1968 replay and not penalties); the biggest margin was the 4–0 of 2012.
  • The tournament grew from 4 teams in 1960 to 24 since 2016: each expansion (8 in 1980, 16 in 1996, 24 in 2016) multiplied the number of matches.
  • Only 1 nation has won two editions in a row: Spain (2008 and 2012).

Titles per nation

West Germany and Germany are counted together (1972, 1980 and 1996); the Soviet Union, Czechoslovakia and Yugoslavia no longer exist as national teams, so their titles and finals belong to no one today.

Finalists who never won

England (2 finals), Yugoslavia (2 finals), Belgium (1 final), Czech Republic (1 final).

It is the flip side of the roll of honour: England and Yugoslavia (2 finals each) have reached the most finals without winning. England played two in a row (2020 and 2024) and lost both.

The tournament has grown: teams per edition

Between 1960 and 1976 only four teams played the final tournament (semi-finals and final); since 1980 there has been a group stage, since 1996 there have been 16 teams and since 2016, 24. More teams brought more variety of champions: Greece (2004) and Denmark (1992, who came in late after Yugoslavia's exclusion) are the two great surprises.

How we made this study

A program calculates the figures from the 17 finals (1960–2024) using UEFA.com data. We count West Germany and Germany as a single nation in the ranking. Euro 2020 was played in 2021 and in 11 countries, so we do not count it as a “host champion” even though Italy staged matches. The 1968 goal count uses the replay (2–0); those of 1976 and 2020 exclude the penalties. The interpretation is TIKITEXTO's; if you spot a mistake, write to us from the contact page.

Frequently asked questions

Which nation has won the most European Championships?

Spain, with 4 titles (1964, 2008, 2012 and 2024); Germany follows with 3, counting those of West Germany.

How many hosts have won the European Championship?

3: 1964, 1968, 1984 (Spain, Italy and France).

How many European Championship finals have gone to penalties?

2: 1976 (Czechoslovakia against West Germany) and 2020 (Italy against England).

How many teams play in the European Championship?

Since 2016, 24. Before that there were 16 (1996–2012), 8 (1980–1992) and 4 (1960–1976).

Which nations have reached a European Championship final without ever winning?

England (2), Yugoslavia (2), Belgium (1), Czech Republic (1).
