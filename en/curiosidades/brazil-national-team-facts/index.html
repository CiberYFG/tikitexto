Football trivia

Brazil national team facts and curiosities

Data as of 2 November 2026

Brazil is the national team with the most World Cups in history and the only one to have played in every edition. Here are some of its most curious stories.

1

Five World Cups

Brazil have won five World Cups: 1958, 1962, 1970, 1994 and 2002. They are the most successful team in the competition's history.

2

The only team at every World Cup

Brazil is the only national team that has taken part in every World Cup edition since 1930, without missing a single one.

3

The Maracanazo

In 1950, in the last match of the World Cup played in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil needed a draw against Uruguay to be champion. They lost 2-1 in front of almost 174,000 paying spectators, a defeat known as the «Maracanazo».

4

From white to yellow

Until 1950, Brazil played in white. After the Maracanazo, a newspaper, Correio da Manhã, held a contest with the federation's backing to create a kit in the colours of the national flag: that is how the yellow shirt with green details was born, first worn in 1954.

5

Pelé, champion at seventeen

At the 1958 World Cup in Sweden, Pelé was 17 and scored a hat-trick in the semi-final and two goals in the final (5-2 against Sweden). He remains the youngest world champion in history.

6

The 1970 team

The team of Pelé, Jairzinho, Rivelino, Tostão and Carlos Alberto won all six matches of the 1970 World Cup and beat Italy 4-1 in the final, with a third title that gave them the Jules Rimet trophy.

Advertisement
7

Cafu, three finals in a row

Full-back Cafu is the only player who has played in three consecutive World Cup finals: 1994, 1998 and 2002. He won two of them, and in 2002 he was the captain who lifted the trophy.

8

The 7-1 in 2014

In the 2014 World Cup semi-final, played in Belo Horizonte, Germany beat Brazil 7-1, Brazil's heaviest defeat at a World Cup. It is known as the «Mineirazo».

9

Nine Copa Américas

Brazil have won nine Copa Américas, the latest in 2019 on home soil after beating Peru 3-1 in the final at the Maracanã.

Frequently asked questions

How many World Cups has Brazil won?

Five: 1958, 1962, 1970, 1994 and 2002.

What was the Maracanazo?

Brazil's 2-1 defeat against Uruguay in the last match of the 1950 World Cup at the Maracanã stadium.

Why does Brazil wear yellow?

It played in white until 1950. After the Maracanazo a contest was held and the yellow shirt with green details was chosen.
