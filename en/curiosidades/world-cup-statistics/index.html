World Cup statistics 1930–2026: champions, hosts, finals and top scorers

Data as of 19 July 2026

TIKITEXTO's own study of the 23 World Cups: nations with most titles, how many hosts won, finals decided in extra time or penalties, the tournament's growth and top scorers' goals. With a filterable table.

The key findings in 6 numbers

  • Brazil is the nation with most titles (5), followed by Italy (4), West Germany (3).
  • Only 6 of 23 hosts won the title at home: Uruguay 1930, Italy 1934, England 1966, West Germany 1974, Argentina 1978, France 1998.
  • Europe has 13 titles and South America 10: no team from another continent has ever won.
  • 6 finals were settled in extra time (1934, 1966, 1978, 2010, 2014, 2026) and 3 on penalties (1994, 2006, 2022).
  • The top scorer averaged 8.1 goals before 1982 and 6.5 since: more matches, but also better-organised defences.
  • The record for a top scorer is 13 goals, in 1958 (Just Fontaine).

Titles per nation

West Germany and Germany are counted together for this comparison (1954, 1974, 1990 and 2014).

The tournament has grown: teams per edition

From 13 teams in 1930 it went to 16 (1934), 24 (1982), 32 (1998) and 48 (2026): each expansion raised the number of matches and the places for the confederations.

Goals by the top scorer at each World Cup

In 1962 six players tied on 4 goals, and the 1994 figure belongs to two players on 6. The peak was Fontaine's 13 in 1958; next came Kocsis with 11 in 1954.

How we made this study

A program computes the figures from the 23 editions on our every World Cup, edition by edition page, with data from FIFA.com. We count West Germany and Germany as one nation in the title ranking. Final goals do not include penalty shoot-outs. The interpretation is by TIKITEXTO.

Frequently asked questions

How many hosts have won the World Cup?

6 of 23: Uruguay 1930, Italy 1934, England 1966, West Germany 1974, Argentina 1978, France 1998.

Which continent has won the most World Cups?

Europe, with 13 titles against South America's 10. No team from any other continent has ever won.

How many World Cup finals have gone to penalties?

3: 1994, 2006, 2022.

How many nations have won the World Cup?

9 nations, counting West Germany and Germany as one: Brazil (5), Italy (4), West Germany (3), Argentina (3), Spain (2), France (2), Uruguay (2), Germany (1), England (1).

Data source: FIFA.com · Copa Mundial de la FIFA. Data as of 19 July 2026.
