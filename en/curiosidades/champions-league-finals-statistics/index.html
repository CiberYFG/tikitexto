Champions League final statistics 2007–2026: clubs, countries and attendance

Data as of 31 October 2026

TIKITEXTO's own study of the last 20 Champions League finals: who wins most, which countries dominate, how many go to penalties, goals per final and average attendance. With a filterable table.

The key findings in 6 numbers

  • Real Madrid won 6 of the 20 finals: 30 % of the titles of these two decades.
  • Spain has 9 titles, followed by England (5).
  • 4 finals were decided on penalties (2008, 2012, 2016, 2026) and 1 in extra time.
  • The average is 2.65 goals per final (53 in 20), not counting penalties.
  • 6 of 20 finals were decided by a single goal (shoot-outs included). The biggest margin was the 5-0 of 2025.
  • Average attendance, excluding 2020 (behind closed doors) and 2021 (limited capacity), was 69,710 spectators.

Final attendance

It peaked in 2011 (Wembley, London: 87,695) and was lowest, among full-stadium finals, in 2014 (60,976). 2020 (behind closed doors, Lisbon) and 2021 (14,110 fans in Porto) are left out of the chart and the average.

How we made this study

Every figure on this page is computed by a program from the 20 finals listed on our last 20 finals page, whose scores, venues and attendances come from UEFA.com. Goals count those scored in play (90 minutes and extra time), not in penalty shoot-outs. The interpretation is by TIKITEXTO; if you spot an error, write to us from the contact page.

Frequently asked questions

Which club has won the most Champions League finals since 2007?

Real Madrid, with 6 titles from the 20 finals played between 2007 and 2026, and it also won every final it played in that period.

How many Champions League finals have gone to penalties?

Of the last 20 finals (2007–2026), 4 were decided on penalties: 2008, 2012, 2016, 2026.

How many goals are scored on average in a Champions League final?

In the 20 finals from 2007 to 2026 the average is 2.65 goals per final, not counting penalties.

Which country has won the most Champions Leagues in the last 20 years?

Spain, with 9 titles (Real Madrid and Barcelona), followed by England with 5.
