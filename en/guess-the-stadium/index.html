Guess the football (soccer) stadium

One hidden stadium and six tries to find it. Choose between stadiums from the Spanish league, the English league, Europe or the rest of the world, with hints about the city and its history.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Type a name with the number of letters shown by the squares.
  2. Press Enter: green = letter in the right place, yellow = it is elsewhere in the name, grey = not in the name.
  3. You have 6 attempts. If you get stuck, use the hint.

Frequently asked questions

Which stadiums are included?

Legendary grounds from Spain and England, big European stadiums and World Cup venues from around the world.

Can I choose the competition?

Yes. When you tap Stadiums in the game you choose between the Spanish league, the English league, Europe or the rest of the world.

Is it free? Do I need to sign up?

It is free and asks for no email or password: you just choose a nickname for the world ranking. A recovery code is kept in case you change device.

How often is there a new challenge?

The daily Tikitexto changes every day and is the same for everyone. You can play the other modes as many times as you like.
