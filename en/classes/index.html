Frequently asked questions

Can I watch it in other languages?

Yes. The animated lesson can be watched in Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Italian and German from the player itself.

Where do the rules come from?

From the IFAB Laws of the Game and FIFA and UEFA regulations. They are reviewed when the rules change, usually every summer.
