What the lesson explains

Cards are how the referee punishes misconduct. Today we'll see what earns a yellow and what earns a red.

A yellow card is a caution. It is shown for a careless foul, unsporting behaviour, dissent towards the referee, delaying the restart of play, not respecting the distance at a wall or a corner, or entering or leaving the pitch without permission.

Careful: two yellow cards in the same match equal a red card. The player leaves the pitch and his team is down to ten, with no replacement allowed.

A straight red is shown for serious foul play, violent conduct, spitting at or biting someone, offensive or insulting words or gestures, or denying a goal or an obvious goalscoring opportunity with a foul or a handball.

On tackles, the key is intensity. If it's careless or reckless, yellow. If excessive force is used and the opponent is put in danger, red.

And remember: in many competitions, collecting yellow cards across different matches leads to a suspension. The exact number depends on each tournament.

Now you know when each card is shown. Let's see if the referee gets it right in the next match!
