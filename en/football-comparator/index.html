How does it work?

Frequently asked questions

Where does the comparator data come from?

From UEFA and FIFA official records, France Football for the Ballon d'Or and federation websites; height and weight are approximate profile values. The data date is shown under the scoreboard.

Which statistics are compared?

For players: Ballon d'Or, Champions League, World Cups, continental titles, Golden Shoes, Champions League and national-team goals and appearances, height, weight and birth year. For clubs, national teams and managers: titles, finals and honours.

Why do some rows show a dash?

When we could not confirm a figure with a reliable source we prefer not to show it; those rows do not score.

Can I share a comparison?

Yes: the Share button copies a link that opens exactly the same duel.
