3D Slalom: football dribbling runner

An endless 3D runner through a stadium: your player runs on his own and you decide the lane, the jump and the slide. Dodge defenders, jump mini-goals and rows of balls, slide under banners and collect coins and golden boots.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Swipe left or right to change lane: one gesture, one lane.
  2. Swipe up to jump and down to slide (or arrow keys, WASD and space on a computer).
  3. Jump tackles and barriers, slide under the “GOOOL!” banners and dodge defenders.
  4. Collect coins (+10) and golden boots for a 6-second turbo.
  5. You can take a yellow card; the red one ends the run.

Rules and scoring

  • Speed grows with distance and day turns into sunset and night every 700 metres.
  • Every row of obstacles always leaves a free path; there are no impossible situations.
  • With the golden-boot turbo you run 35 % faster and knock obstacles over.
  • A yellow card gives you almost two seconds of invulnerability; the second one (red) ends the run.
  • Passing 500 metres earns +1 tikitexto once per run.
  • The whole pitch, the netted barriers, the rows of balls, the banners and the corner flags are drawn with our own code, with no real brands or crests. The game also adapts its resolution on slower phones so that it keeps running smoothly, and the motion effects switch off if your device asks for reduced motion.

Tips to play better

  • Look beyond the nearest obstacle: choose the lane in advance instead of reacting late.
  • Do not change lane right before a jump; jump first, move after.
  • Defenders cannot be jumped: change lane. Barriers and rows of balls can be jumped.
  • Save the turbo for stretches with many obstacles: knocking them down saves you manoeuvres.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play in portrait?

Yes, it works in both portrait and landscape.

What are the cards?

Your lives: two hits and the run is over.

Can obstacles be impossible?

No, the generator guarantees a free path.

Is there a distance limit?

No, it is endless; speed grows up to a maximum.
