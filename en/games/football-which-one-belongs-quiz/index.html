What does it belong to? Football quiz with 4 options

We show a player, a club or a stadium and four options. You choose the right one: his national team, its country, his position or the club he played for. With every round the questions get harder, the traps more alike and the time shorter.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Read the question and look at the name shown on top.
  2. Pick one of the four options before time runs out.
  3. Each correct answer adds points, a speed bonus and a streak of up to ×10.
  4. Three mistakes end the game.
  5. Save your best score and keep climbing levels.

Rules and scoring

  • There are different question types: stadium→country, club→country, player→national team, position, World Cup winner, club he played for, Ballon d’Or and “who passed through this club?”. At high levels you will see “which club did he NOT play for?”.
  • Every three rounds the difficulty rises; the time per question falls from 15 seconds to a minimum of 6.
  • There is always exactly one correct option, because the club and award lists of each player are complete.
  • Points: 100 per correct answer, plus a time bonus and 10 for each answer in a row (maximum 10).
  • You earn +1 tikitexto at 12 correct answers and another one at 25.

Tips to play better

  • Answer the easy ones fast to save margin for the hard ones: the time bonus counts.
  • In “which club he did NOT play for”, rule out the clubs you are sure about first; the one left is the answer.
  • Learn the national teams and positions of the big names: they appear a lot.
  • If you hesitate between two, think about which trap the game set: they are usually from the same country or position.

Frequently asked questions

How many mistakes are allowed?

Three. On the third mistake the game ends.

Is the time always the same?

No, it falls with each round from 15 down to 6 seconds.

Can there be two correct answers?

No: questions are generated so that there is only one.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in your profile (guest or account).
