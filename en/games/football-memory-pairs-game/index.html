Football memory pairs: match players, clubs, stadiums and countries

TIKITEXTO Memory Pairs mixes the classic face-down card game with your football knowledge. Every board hides several pairs, but they are not two identical cards: you have to recognise which cards go together. Messi with Argentina, Real Madrid with the Santiago Bernabéu, Anfield with England. Before every board all the cards are shown for a few seconds so you can memorise them, then they are turned over. There is a time bank: each miss takes a few seconds off and each completed board gives you more. Boards grow from 12 to 24 cards and the memorising time gets shorter, so the longer you last the harder it gets.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Press “Play”: you start with 80 seconds in the time bank.
  2. Memorise the cards shown for a few seconds at the start of every board.
  3. Flip two cards: if they form a pair they stay face up and score points and streak.
  4. If they are not a pair they turn back and you lose 3 seconds.
  5. Complete all the pairs to move to the next board, which is bigger and gives you extra time.

Rules and scoring

  • Valid pairs: player with his national team, club with its stadium, club with its country and stadium with its country. No other relation counts (for example, a player with a club is not a pair).
  • Every board has two modes that alternate: “Player ↔ national team” and “Clubs, stadiums and countries”. It starts with 6 pairs (12 cards) and rises to 12 pairs (24 cards).
  • Time bank: 80 seconds at the start (maximum 120). Each miss takes off 3 seconds and the peek (once per game) takes off 6. Completing a board adds between 18 and 46 seconds.
  • Points: 100 per pair plus 25 for each consecutive pair (up to 8 extra) and, when the board is complete, 300 plus 50 per board and 3 per second left over.
  • Reward: +1 tikitext for completing the second board and another for the fifth in the same game.

Tips to play better

  • Watch the border colours: each type of card has its own (players, clubs, stadiums and countries) and they tell you which types can pair with each other.
  • Memorise in blocks: group the cards by country instead of remembering every single position.
  • Use the peek when you have half the board done: it costs 6 seconds, but it shows you the remaining cards again.
  • Do not flip cards at random: a miss costs 3 seconds and breaks your streak.

Frequently asked questions

Which pairs are valid?

Player with his national team, club with its stadium, club with its country and stadium with its country. A player with his club does not count as a pair.

How long do I have to memorise?

About 3 seconds on the first board; the memorising time gets shorter on the following ones.

What happens if I miss?

Both cards turn back and you lose 3 seconds from the time bank. Your streak of consecutive pairs is also lost.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest; with an account (from age 14) your tikitexts count towards the world ranking.
