Football connections puzzle: group players, clubs and stadiums

Quartets is a football logic puzzle: you get 16 tiles with names of players, clubs and stadiums and must sort them into four groups of four that share something. There is a daily challenge, the same for everybody, plus free-play rounds.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Tap four tiles you think form a group and confirm.
  2. If you are right, the group is revealed with its title (for example “Played for Milan” or “Stadiums of Italy”).
  3. If you are wrong, you lose one of your 4 lives and the tiles are deselected.
  4. You have two hints, each revealing the title of one group.
  5. Solve all four groups before you run out of mistakes.

Rules and scoring

  • Groups can be players of the same national team, players who passed through one club or through two specific clubs, a position, Ballon d’Or winners or World Cup winners, clubs of one country or stadiums of one country.
  • Each puzzle is generated and checked so that only one valid split exists, even if some names seem to fit two groups.
  • The daily challenge uses the date as a seed, so everybody gets the same puzzle and there is one attempt per day; you can share your result.
  • Winning the daily challenge earns +1 tikitexto, and in free play you earn +1 every three wins.

Tips to play better

  • Start with the most specific group: a single club or a position usually has fewer candidates than “clubs of a country”.
  • Beware of names that fit two groups: if in doubt, solve first the groups that share no tiles.
  • Think about careers: a player can appear in “played for” one club and also in “passed through” two clubs.
  • Spend hints only when you are out of ideas; one revealed group narrows the rest of the board a lot.

Frequently asked questions

How many mistakes are allowed?

Four. On the fourth mistake the round ends and the groups are shown.

Can I replay the daily challenge?

No: there is one attempt per day for everybody. Afterwards you can continue in free play.

Do I need an account?

No, you can play as a guest. With an account your progress counts for the ranking.

Where does the data come from?

From our own lists of players, clubs and stadiums, checked against official sources and updated every summer.
