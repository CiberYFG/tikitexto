Find the Country: a blank world map with a daily challenge

Find the Country is a blank world map with two kinds of round. In “tap on the map” we ask for a country by name, the map appears without any labels and you have to move it, zoom in and tap the right territory; in “highlighted country” a country shows up with its outline marked in gold and you must pick its name among four. The countries are spread across all the continents and get harder as the game goes on: you start with big, well-known ones and finish with small or less familiar ones. After each round the right country lights up, and if you tapped another one it is marked in red, so you learn where each one is.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Press “Today's challenge” (the same for everyone, one attempt a day) or “Free game” to practise.
  2. If the round is a tap round, read the country being asked for, move the map with your finger or mouse and zoom in until you find it.
  3. Tap the map to place the pin (you can change it by tapping another spot) and press “Confirm”.
  4. If the round is a highlighted-country round, look at its shape and size and tap the name you think is right among the four options.
  5. You will see the right country lit up and, if you tapped another one, the one you chose too. Press “Next” until the five rounds are done.

Rules and scoring

  • A game is five rounds of increasing difficulty: two very well-known countries, two intermediate ones and one hard one. Tap rounds and highlighted rounds are mixed.
  • In tap rounds you have 30 seconds and a correct answer gives between 600 and 1,000 points depending on speed. If you miss narrowly — your tap lands 120 kilometres or less from the border of the country asked — you get 250 points.
  • In highlighted-country rounds you have 22 seconds and a correct answer gives between 500 and 1,000 points; a miss scores 0.
  • The outlines come from Natural Earth (public domain), simplified so the map loads fast: the game checks which country your tap falls in, so a tap right on a shore or a very thin border can land on either side. A tap on the sea does not count as a country.
  • In the daily challenge you earn a tikitexto if your average is above 500 points per round; in free games, one every three games with an average above 600.

Tips to play better

  • Start by locating the continent and zoom in from there: enlarging a region makes small countries stop being a dot.
  • Use the neighbours: if you are asked for Uruguay, look for Brazil and Argentina first. Almost every country is found by reference to a bigger one.
  • In highlighted rounds, look at the shape first: a long peninsula, an island or a boot-shaped country is recognised before its exact position.
  • Tapping a little outside is not lost: within 120 kilometres of the border you still get 250 points.
  • Review the countries you miss: when the round ends the right one lights up with its name, just when it is easiest to remember.

Frequently asked questions

How many countries are there?

Seventy-four from every continent, from the big, well-known ones to smaller or less familiar ones. The list is split into three difficulty levels and each game has two easy, two intermediate and one hard country.

How does the game know where I tapped?

The country outlines are in our own file built from Natural Earth data. When you confirm, the game checks which outline the point you tapped falls in.

Can I change the pin?

Yes. Tap another spot on the map and the pin moves. It only counts when you press “Confirm”.

Is it the same challenge for everyone?

Yes. Today's challenge comes from the local date: everybody gets the same countries and options, with one attempt a day and a result you can share without revealing the countries.

Are the names shown in my language?

Yes. Country names appear in the language you have chosen: Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Italian or German.
