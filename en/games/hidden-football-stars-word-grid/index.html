Hidden Stars: find footballers in the letter grid

A 5×6 grid of letters hides several names from one theme: club legends, mythical stadiums, goalkeepers, Ballon d’Or winners… Slide your finger from letter to letter, diagonals included, to form each name before time runs out.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Read the grid theme, for example “Barça legends”.
  2. Slide your finger (or tap letters one by one) joining neighbouring letters.
  3. If the name is one of the hidden ones, it is marked and scores points with a combo.
  4. When you complete all the names, you move on to a new grid and gain 45 seconds.
  5. If you are stuck, use a hint or “Show answers”.

Rules and scoring

  • Each grid contains between 4 and 7 names that add up to exactly the 30 letters: every letter belongs to some name.
  • The clock starts at 180 seconds (maximum 240) and each completed grid adds 45.
  • Hints are infinite and progressive: they circle the next letter of a name and every press reveals one more. They cost 6 seconds, or 3 if pressed in a row.
  • “Show answers” reveals all names for 10 seconds.
  • You earn +1 tikitexto when you complete the second grid and another one on the fifth.

Tips to play better

  • Look first for long names or those with rare letters (J, Z, K): they are easier to spot.
  • Names never overlap: if a letter is already part of one, do not use it in another.
  • Paths can turn and cross diagonally; do not think only in rows and columns.
  • When you see the theme, run through the list in your head: you usually know half of the names.

Frequently asked questions

How many themes are there?

More than thirty: teams of the big leagues, stadiums, legends by club and by national team, goalkeepers, strikers and Ballon d’Or winners.

Are accents used?

No, all names use letters from A to Z, with no accents or spaces.

What if I find the name along another path?

The game warns you: only the path designed for that grid counts.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it is made for swiping, and you can also play with a mouse.
