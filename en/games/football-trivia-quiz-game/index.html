Football trivia quiz: 10 questions on the Champions League, World Cups and Ballon d'Or

TIKITEXTO Trivia is a football question-and-answer game for anyone who enjoys remembering finals, champions and awards. Each round has ten questions with four possible answers and fifteen seconds to reply, and the difficulty rises as you go: four easy questions, four of medium level and two that are genuinely hard. The questions are not written at random: they are generated from the data we publish on the site, such as the European Cup and Champions League winners since 1956, every World Cup edition since 1930, the complete Ballon d'Or list and the records in the Comparator. That means every answer can be checked on our history and data pages, which cite sources such as UEFA, FIFA and France Football.

How to play

  1. Pick “Today's challenge” or free play: in the challenge everybody plays the same ten questions and you only get one attempt a day; in free play you can choose Champions League, World Cups, Ballon d'Or, Records or a mix.
  2. Read the question and tap one of the four answers (or use the A, B, C and D keys, or 1, 2, 3 and 4). The bar at the top counts the fifteen seconds.
  3. If you are unsure, use the 50:50 lifeline once per game (button at the top or the F key): it removes two wrong answers.
  4. After each answer you see the solution and, when there is data, a card with the final result, the host or the top scorer so you learn a little more. Tap “Next” or wait a moment.
  5. At the end you get a rank, from Substitute to Legend, and a grid of green and red squares you can share without giving away the questions.

Rules and scoring

  • Each correct answer is worth 100 points plus up to 50 bonus for speed and 10 extra for each answer in a row of your streak (up to 50). A wrong answer or running out of time breaks the streak but does not remove points.
  • A round always has ten questions ordered from easier to harder. No question repeats within the same game and, where possible, no question type appears more than twice.
  • Ranks: fewer than 4 correct, Substitute; 4 to 6, Starter; 7 or 8, Captain; 9, Star player; 10 out of 10, Legend.
  • Reward: in Today's challenge, 7 or more correct earns +1 tikitext (once a day). In free play, a perfect 10 out of 10 earns +1 tikitext. Playing as a guest counts too.
  • The daily challenge resets at midnight on your device's clock and your streak of consecutive days is saved.

Tips to play better

  • Recent World Cups and Champions League finals are easier to remember: revise the latest champions and finalists and the points from the first questions are almost guaranteed.
  • When a numbers question throws you, discard the extreme options first: football records usually sit in the middle of the list.
  • Keep the 50:50 lifeline for one of the last two questions, which are the hardest; using it early wastes its value.
  • After each game read the cards: every final and every World Cup comes with its score, host and top scorer, and what you learn will help in the next challenge.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the questions come from?

They are generated automatically from the data on the site: European Cup and Champions League winners, World Cups, the Ballon d'Or and the figures in the Comparator. Sources are UEFA, FIFA, France Football and official websites.

Are the Today's challenge questions the same for everyone?

Yes. They are chosen with a seed based on the date, so every player gets the same ten questions in the same order with the same options.

Can I repeat Today's challenge?

No: there is one attempt a day, but you can look at your result again and share it. To keep playing use free play, which has no limit.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play as a guest; if you create an account (from age 14), your tikitexts count towards the world ranking.
