Data duel: who has more? The football stats comparison game

Data duel is a football-intuition game built on real numbers. Two names appear on screen, for example two players, two clubs or two managers, together with a question: who has more Ballons d’Or, who has scored more Champions League goals, who has won more World Cups? You choose before time runs out and, once you answer, both figures are revealed. The questions come from the same tables as the TIKITEXTO Comparator, so every game is also a fun way to learn facts from football history.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Read the question on top: it tells you which statistic is being compared, for example “Who has more Champions League titles?”.
  2. Tap the card of one of the two names (or use the ← and → keys, or keys 1 and 2) before the time bar reaches zero.
  3. When you answer, both figures are revealed: the correct card lights up green and, if you were wrong, yours turns red.
  4. Each correct answer adds points; each mistake (or running out of time) costs you one of your three balls.
  5. Keep going until you run out of balls and save your best score.

Rules and scoring

  • Pairs are drawn from the players, clubs, national teams and managers of the Comparator, and only figures with a verified value are compared: you are never asked about an unknown number.
  • There are no ties: pairs with the same value are discarded, so there is always exactly one correct answer.
  • Every three rounds the level goes up (there are six). At the start the gap between the two is large (for example, 8 Ballons d’Or against 1); at high levels it can be a single title or a few goals.
  • The time per question falls from 12 seconds to a minimum of 5.
  • Points: 100 per correct answer, up to 100 more for speed and 10 per consecutive correct answer (up to ×10). You earn +1 tikitext when you reach 10 and 25 correct answers in one game.
  • The figures are those of the Comparator, with their update date in view; if a number changes with the season, it is updated there and in the game.

Tips to play better

  • Big honours lists are easy to remember: Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo dominate the Ballons d’Or, Real Madrid the Champions League and Brazil the World Cups. Use them as reference points.
  • At high levels, compare with context: a 1990s striker played fewer European games than a modern one, and that explains many differences.
  • The national teams with the most World Cup titles are not always the ones that score the most World Cup goals: tell titles apart from accumulated figures.
  • If in doubt, watch the clock: answering fast gives more points, but a mistake costs a ball. Sometimes it pays to wait one more second.
  • Between games, open the Comparator and review the numbers of the names that gave you the most trouble.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the figures come from?

From the TIKITEXTO Comparator, which gathers data from UEFA, FIFA, France Football, RSSSF and official league and club websites. Each table shows its update date.

How many mistakes are allowed?

Three. On the third the game ends and your best score is saved.

Can the two names be tied?

No. The game discards pairs with the same value so that there is always exactly one correct answer.

Do I need an account?

No, you can play as a guest. With an account, your tikitexts count for the world ranking.

Why are there names I do not know?

Because managers and national teams are compared too. Player names are never translated and the figures always give you the answer at the end of the round.
