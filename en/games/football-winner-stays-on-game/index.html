Winner stays on: pick the best among 16 football legends

Winner Stays On turns the classic “who is better” debate into an elimination game. The game picks 16 legends at random from its list, pits them against each other two at a time and you decide who wins each duel. The winner stays on and faces the next rival, so a player can chain several wins in a row, just like in the playground when the winning team keeps playing. After fifteen duels only one champion is left: yours. There are no right or wrong answers, because it is a matter of opinion: on each card you see the national team, the position, the clubs the player went through and whether he won the Ballon d'Or or the World Cup. You can play with all the players or limit yourself to forwards, midfielders, defenders and goalkeepers, Ballon d'Or winners or world champions, and at the end you share your podium with whoever you want.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Choose a category (all, forwards, midfielders, defenders and goalkeepers, Ballon d'Or or world champions) and press “Play”.
  2. You will see two cards with each player's data: national team, position, clubs and awards.
  3. Tap the card of the one you think is better (or use the ← → keys or 1 and 2 on a computer).
  4. The winner stays on and faces the next player; the loser is out.
  5. After 15 duels your champion appears with a podium of the three who won the most duels, ready to share.

Rules and scoring

  • Each game uses 16 different players from the game's list, picked at random within the category you have chosen.
  • The first duel pits the first two players against each other; after that the winner faces each of the remaining ones until 15 duels are completed.
  • There is no correct answer: it is a personal vote. That is why it gives no tikitexts and does not affect the ranking; the game is there to argue with your friends.
  • The podium puts your champion first and then the players with the most duels won; in case of a tie the order in which they appeared counts.
  • The statistics saved on your device record games played, champions crowned and your best streak of duels won by a single player.

Tips to play better

  • Decide on a fixed criterion before starting (honours, pure talent, longevity or impact on a team) and keep it for all 15 duels: that way your champion will be consistent.
  • Look at the awards on the card: the Ballon d'Or and the World Cup are a good tiebreaker when you hesitate between two players.
  • Try the categories: “Defenders and goalkeepers” or “Midfielders” give much less obvious duels than forwards.
  • Play with a friend on the same screen and vote in turns: the funniest debates come when you disagree.
  • Share your podium to see how many agree with you; two people's podiums almost never match.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a correct answer in each duel?

No. It is a matter of opinion: you decide who is better according to the criterion you prefer. That is why there are no points or tikitexts.

How many duels does a game have?

Fifteen: the first duel pits two players against each other and the winner then faces the other fourteen, one by one.

Which players appear?

Players from the game's list: in “All” you get those who have won the Ballon d'Or or the World Cup or passed through three or more big clubs; in the other categories, those of that position or that award.

Can I play with the keyboard?

Yes: use the left and right arrows or the 1 and 2 keys to pick each player.

Is my choice sent to any server?

No. The game runs on your device and only saves your statistics there; the text to share is generated by you when you press the button.
