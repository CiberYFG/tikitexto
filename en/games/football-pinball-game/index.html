Golazo Pinball: football pinball with a tournament

A pinball with a stadium soul: the ball is the football, the flippers are two boots, the bumpers are defenders and at the top a goal waits with a moving keeper. Beat four tournament rounds, from the round of 16 to the final.

Play now →

How to play

  1. Hold the launch lane and release to shoot the ball (Space on a computer).
  2. Use the left and right boots (screen halves, arrow keys or Z and M) to hit.
  3. Bounce the ball off defenders, posts and targets and send it to the goal.
  4. Complete the T-I-K-I traps to multiply from ×2 to ×5.
  5. Score the goals of each round before the clock runs out.

Rules and scoring

  • A goal only counts if the ball has touched something else on the table first.
  • Rounds: Round of 16 (2 goals in 60 s), Quarter-finals (3 in 60 s), Semi-final (4 in 65 s) and Final (5 in 70 s).
  • You start with 3 balls and gain one more per round passed, up to five; if they run out or the clock hits zero, you are eliminated.
  • Each round the keeper grows and moves faster. You have one VAR per game to replay the first ball if it drops early.
  • You earn +1 tikitexto for passing the Quarter-finals and another for winning the Final.

Tips to play better

  • Try completing T-I-K-I before chasing goals: the multiplier makes each goal worth much more.
  • Hold the ball with the boot for a moment to aim before hitting.
  • Do not waste the ball on the launch: medium power is usually enough to enter play.
  • With the big keeper, aim at the sides; central shots are the ones he saves most.

Frequently asked questions

How many rounds does the tournament have?

Four: round of 16, quarter-final, semi-final and final.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes: tap the left or right half to flip each boot.

What is the game’s VAR?

A replay of the first ball if it is lost in the first 11 seconds, once per game.

How many balls can be in play at once?

Up to three with the multiball.
