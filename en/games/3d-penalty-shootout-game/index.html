3D penalty shootout: play as kicker and goalkeeper

A full shootout in a 3D stadium: you take your penalties by aiming at the goal and judging the power, and you save the rival’s by choosing where to dive. Five each and, if tied, sudden death.

Play now →

How to play

  1. When you shoot, tap the goal to aim: the yellow dots mark your target.
  2. Press to stop the power bar as close as possible to the green zone.
  3. When you save, choose one of the six zones (top or bottom × left, centre or right) by tapping or with Q W E / A S D.
  4. Shots alternate and the winner is whoever scores more after five, or the first to lead in sudden death.
  5. Pick the difficulty: easy, normal, hard or legend.

Rules and scoring

  • Aiming error depends on how far from the green zone you stop the bar; the zone is very narrow and passes quickly.
  • The rival keeper reads your shot, but he only saves it if his body actually reaches the ball: posts and crossbar bounce it away.
  • When you are the keeper, a save counts if the ball ends in the zone you chose (with 35 cm of margin at the edges).
  • On mobile you must rotate the phone to landscape; on a computer it plays fullscreen.
  • You earn +1 tikitexto for every shootout won and your stats are saved.

Tips to play better

  • Vary the zone: a kicker who always repeats the same angle becomes easy to save.
  • A corner with a bit less power is better than a missed blast: error grows with distance from the green.
  • As keeper, watch the run-up and the body of the kicker before deciding.
  • Start on easy to learn the bar’s rhythm and move up when you can hit the green.

Frequently asked questions

How many penalties are in a shootout?

Five per side, and sudden death if still tied.

Can I play as goalkeeper?

Yes, in each shootout you alternately shoot and save.

Do I need to download anything?

No, it runs in the browser with nothing to install.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, in landscape. The game warns you if the phone is upright.
